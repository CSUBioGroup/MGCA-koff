## Zhao dataset

The Zhao dataset in `data/` contains the 2,773-record data and split files used
by the released workflows. Its scientific source is Zhao et al., Journal of
Chemical Information and Modeling, DOI:
<https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.4c00726>.

No standalone dataset redistribution license was identified in the materials
packaged with this repository. The data are included for research
reproducibility with source attribution; users remain responsible for
complying with the source publication's terms. This notice does not grant
additional rights to the data.
